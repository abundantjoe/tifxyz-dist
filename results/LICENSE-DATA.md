# Data licence for results/

The files in this directory (per-segment-pair clearance numbers and their summary) are derived from
Vesuvius Challenge data and are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
They contain no CT data and no meshes: only distances and witness coordinates computed from the
public PHerc0172 segment meshes on volume 20241024131838.

Source data citation:

> Giorgio Angelotti, Stephen Parsons, Sean Johnson, Elian Rafael Dal Prà, Johannes Rudolph, Paul Tafforeau, Alessandro Mirone, Paul Henderson, Hendrik Schilling, Forrest McDonald, David Josey, Youssef Nader, C. Seth Parker, W. Brent Seales. *Vesuvius Challenge - CT Scans of Herculaneum Papyri*. Vesuvius Challenge.

https://scrollprize.org/data
